Gubbiscart Privacy Policy

We collect personal information such as your name, email address, shipping address, billing details, and browsing behavior on our platform.
Your data is retained for as long as your account is active, or for up to 5 years after your last transaction for legal and tax purposes.
We do not sell your personal data to any third-party marketing agencies or data brokers.
To request the deletion of your account and associated personal data, please contact customer support at privacy@gubbicart.com.
Data deletion requests are typically processed and completed within 30 days of receiving your email.
