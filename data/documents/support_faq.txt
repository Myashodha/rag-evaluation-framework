GubbisCart Support Policy

Our customer support team is available from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST (Indian Standard Time).
You can reach out to us via Email (support@gubbicart.com), Live Chat (available on our website), or Phone (1-800-GUBBICART).
We aim to respond to all Email inquiries within 2 hours. Live Chat and Phone calls are typically answered within 5 minutes.
Support is available on Saturdays from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. We are closed on Sundays and major public holidays.
